Dapur Kilat — Sizzle & Serve: a fast-paced cooking game. Orders keep coming in on the ticket board — chop the vegetables on the board, cook patties and eggs on the two-burner stove (catch the green perfect window, never burn), stack each dish on the plate and ring the bell to serve before the customer walks away. Eight original Indonesian recipes (Burger Kilat, Nasi Goreng, Mie Goreng, Ayam Geprek and more), combo tips, a 150-second shift and a 120-coin target. v3 redraws the whole kitchen to look delicious: glossy food with highlights and sparkles, saturated teal-and-orange warung colours, and customers you can actually see — each with a face, a mood and a patience bar. Keyboard and mouse, offline, no build step — just open game/index.html in desktop Chrome.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — or open game/index.html directly in a desktop browser.
